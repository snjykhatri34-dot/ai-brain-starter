# Monthly Brain review

```text
Review my whole Brain (every file in brain/). For each file, tell me:

1. Stale: facts that are likely out of date, especially anything with a "Last reviewed" date more than 60 days old, or goals whose deadline has passed.
2. Conflicts: places where two files disagree.
3. Bloat: sections that are vague, repeated, or over 600 words per file. Suggest cuts.
4. Gaps: TODOs still open, and anything I've clearly been working on that the Brain doesn't mention.

Put your findings in a table: File | Issue | Suggested change. Then ask me which ones to apply. Don't change anything until I answer.
```
